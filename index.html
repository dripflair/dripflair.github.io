PERSONAL ASSISTANT

Alfred

Alfred is a privately operated personal assistant that helps its owner keep track of upcoming deadlines and dated tasks.

Google Calendar and Tasks

With the owner's permission, Alfred reads Google Calendar and Google Tasks to identify the next upcoming deadline or incomplete dated task. It sends a morning summary to the owner's paired private Telegram chat at 8 a.m. America/New_York.

Access is read-only. Alfred does not create, change, or delete Google events or tasks. The assistant runs on its owner's private server; this website provides information only and does not offer a public sign-in.

Privacy and support

Read the privacy policy for data access, storage, sharing, and deletion information.

Contact the operator through the operator's GitHub profile. Do not post private calendar details or credentials in public messages.
